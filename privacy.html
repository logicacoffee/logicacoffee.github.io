Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026 — Brew Today, freshness, and on-device analysis clarified

Updated policy passages are shown in English while translations are prepared.

Logica Coffee operates no servers and does not collect, sell, or use your personal data. Your record stays on your devices and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account.

Overview

Logica Coffee is a personal brewing companion. No account is required. Journal entries, beans, recipes, settings, tasting sessions, freshness inputs, and the history used by Brew Today are stored on your device. Optional iCloud Documents sync uses your own Apple account; Logica cannot access it.

Brew Today & bean freshness

Brew Today and freshness guidance are calculated on your device from information you record, including bean identity, roast date, selected method, remaining weight, and your own brew history. These results are stored with the app's local data or in your own iCloud when sync is enabled. They are not sent to a Logica server.

Camera, photos & scanning

Bag and cup photos are used only when you take or select them. Apple's photo picker provides only the image you choose. Bag-label text recognition uses Apple's Vision framework on device. Photos remain local or in your own iCloud alongside the records they belong to.

Notifications, Apple Watch & Apple Health

Freshness, brewing, and maintenance reminders are local notifications scheduled by your device after permission. WatchConnectivity shares brew-session information between your iPhone and Apple Watch. Optional caffeine logging writes estimates to Apple Health and does not read your Health data.

On-device brew analysis

Logica's optional feature uses Apple's Foundation Models framework on compatible iPhone and iPad devices running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Availability also requires Apple Intelligence, the on-device model, and locale readiness. You can control it with the On-Device Analysis toggle in Settings when the feature is available.

When Logica's on-device feature runs, brew information stays on device and Logica does not send it to us, Apple, or a Logica server. This statement describes Logica's specific Foundation Models feature; it is not a claim about every Apple Intelligence feature. If analysis is off or unavailable, James uses the deterministic offline engine.

Recipe sharing

When you choose Share, the recipe is encoded in the link or QR code. Logica does not upload the recipe. Opening a link first loads a static GitHub Pages redirect; GitHub may keep ordinary web request logs. A received recipe is previewed before you choose to save it.

System frameworks & services

Logica includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. It uses Apple system services including iCloud Documents, WatchConnectivity, WidgetKit, ActivityKit, HealthKit, Vision, Foundation Models, the system photo picker, and local notifications. Those Apple services operate under Apple's privacy policy.

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or account data
  • No location or cross-app tracking
  • No analytics, advertising profile, or usage statistics
  • No reading of Apple Health data
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Your choices

You can disable iCloud in device settings, turn off On-Device Analysis in Logica Settings when available, revoke notifications or Health access in system settings, delete individual records in the app, or remove the app and its data. Export tools let you keep a copy of your own history.

Children, changes & contact

Logica does not knowingly collect data from users of any age. Material policy changes will update the date on this page before the related release. Questions? Email khatskevich.mv@gmail.com.
